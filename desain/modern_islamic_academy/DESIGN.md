## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the synthesis of classical Islamic scholarship and forward-thinking modern education. The visual language conveys moral integrity, intellectual rigor, institutional prestige, and warm hospitality. It bridges the deep historical spiritual heritage of an Islamic boarding school (*pesantren*) with the clarity, efficiency, and technological competence of an international preparatory academy.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Prospective & Current Parents:** Seek uncompromising trust, moral safety, academic discipline, and organizational transparency.
- **Students & Santri:** Experience an inviting, organized, vibrant academic home that feels simultaneously grounded in faith and oriented toward global futures.
- **Scholars, Alumni & Donors:** Encounter a dignified, enduring legacy worthy of sustained support.

### Aesthetic Movement: Contemporary Heritage
The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutional Minimalism** with **Refined Classical Accents**:
- Vast, uncluttered layouts with deliberate breathing room to inspire calm contemplation.
- Subtle geometric arabesque textures integrated strictly as low-contrast watermarks or structural section delimiters.
- Crisp, hyper-legible structural cards defined by delicate warm-sand borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Balanced duality between modern precision typography and noble editorial serif detailing for spiritual excerpts, hadiths, and institutional mottos.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from sacred architecture, gilded manuscripts, and natural parchment. Contrast ratios strictly adhere to WCAG AAA standards for reading comfort and accessibility.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#14532D` - Forest Emerald):** Symbolizes growth, life, spiritual grounding, and Islamic tradition. Serves as the dominant anchor for primary actions, navigation headers, institutional crests, and key accents.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber Gold):** Evokes illuminated manuscripts, academic honor, and warmth. Applied selectively to callouts, milestone badges, active tabs, and secondary CTAs.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Burnished Ochre):** Adds grounding depth to amber states, hover interactions, and bordered highlights.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Used for primary body text, headlines, and data displays, providing a softer, more refined reading experience than harsh pure black.
- **Neutral Soft (`#475569` - Slate Muted):** Used for supporting descriptions, metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAF9` - Warm Sand/Ivory):** The foundational page background, avoiding clinical blue-white in favor of an organic, welcoming parchment tone.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for interactive cards, dialogs, forms, and floating navigation bars.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0` / `#E7E5E4`):** Soft stone and slate lines framing modular content blocks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between academic authority and modern functional speed.

### Font Pairings
- **Headings & Sacred Script (`Source Serif 4`):** Delivers literary gravitas, scholarly poise, and editorial distinction. Used for main landing page titles, chapter titles, institutional statements, and bilingual Quranic or prophetic translations.
- **Body, UI & Navigation (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures clean, geometric, high-legibility layout construction across admissions portals, curriculum outlines, and complex data tables.
- **Arabic Text Integration:** When rendering Arabic verses or Hadith quotations, pair with an authentic Naskh or Amiri web typeface, displayed at `1.25x` the point size of the adjacent Latin text with a line height of at least `2.0` to accommodate diacritics (*harakat*).

### Rules of Rhythm
- Display and `headline-lg` styles should always employ tight negative letter-spacing for cohesive visual impact.
- Micro-labels and status indicators utilize `label-sm` set in uppercase with widened tracking (+0.05em) for crisp legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a balanced, orderly fixed-width fluid container system that promotes symmetry, focus, and visual peace.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) outer margin padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Sectional Cadence
Major institutional landing sections (e.g., *Vision & Values*, *Curriculum Pillars*, *Admission Pathways*) utilize extensive vertical padding (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem-6rem on desktop) to instill an atmosphere of order, contemplation, and distinction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile surface layering and soft, low-contrast illumination rather than high-contrast drops.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Layering over Shadows:** Surface separation is achieved by resting pristine white cards (`#FFFFFF`) against the subtle sand canvas (`#FAFAF9`), held together by hairline borders (`#E7E5E4`).
- **Ambient Shadow System:** When elevation is necessary for hovering cards or popovers, use deep-emerald-tinted ambient shadows:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04)` with a `1px` border of `#E7E5E4`.
  - *Elevated / Floating Card:* `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.03)`.
  - *Dropdowns & Modals:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Ornamental Backdrop:** Islamic geometric motifs (octagonal 8-point stars and interlaced geometry) must be rendered as SVG vectors at an opacity of `2%` to `4%` against primary or sand surfaces, preventing visual clutter while establishing cultural identity.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric moderation (`roundedness: 2`).

### Corner Language
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Inputs, buttons, segmented controls, table headers, and badges.
- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-lg: 1rem` / 16px):** Standard card containers, media wrappers, and dialog panels.
- **Special Heritage Elements (`rounded-xl: 1.5rem` / 24px):** Hero feature banners, campus tour showcases, and quote callout blocks.
- **Arch Motifs:** As an architectural accent, hero imagery cards and section dividers may feature subtle soft arch geometry (rounded-top arches: top radius set to `4rem` or `6rem`, bottom radius standard `1rem`) reflecting the traditional pesantren dome and gateway entrance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald background (`#14532D`), white text, bold label font, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover transitions to `#0F5132` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary (Accented):** Warm Amber background (`#D97706`), white text. Used exclusively for admission deadlines, open house registration, and critical calls-to-action. Hover transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Outline:** Crisp `1.5px` border in `#14532D` with text in `#14532D` and a transparent background. Hover fills with `#14532D` at `6%` opacity.
- **Text/Ghost:** `#14532D` text with a subtle right-chevron icon for inline links and editorial discovery.

### Cards
- **Institutional Profile Cards:** Pure white surface, `1px` solid `#E7E5E4` perimeter border, `1rem` corner radius, `1.5rem` internal padding. Optional gold hairline top accent bar (`2px` solid `#D97706`) for highlighted programs.
- **Quote / Hadith Banner Cards:** Deep Emerald surface (`#14532D`) with white and amber text, framed with a faint `5%` gold geometric perimeter line and ambient gold corner ornaments.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Pure white container with `1px` border of `#CBD5E1`, text in `#1E293B`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`, and `0.5rem` radius. On focus: border shifts to `#14532D` with a `3px` focus ring of `#14532D` at `15%` opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald active state (`#14532D`) with crisp white interior marks. Focus rings mirror standard input fields.

### Chips & Badges
- **Academic Tags:** Light emerald background (`#DCFCE7`), deep emerald text (`#14532D`), `0.25rem` radius, `label-sm` typography.
- **Admissions / Status Badges:** Soft amber background (`#FEF3C7`), dark amber text (`#92400E`).

### Lists & Curriculum Trees
- **Curriculum & Schedule Lists:** Clean border-bottom dividers (`#F1F5F9`), left-aligned emerald indicators or numbering set in `Source Serif 4`, and slate subheadings for subject details.

### Specialized Component: Prayer & Academic Schedule Widget
- Compact tabular card with real-time prayer schedule alongside standard academic classes. Uses amber indicator dots to represent the upcoming event, accompanied by a soft slate countdown badge.
